1. Who we are

This website, sollombok.com, is operated by Sol Properties ("we", "us", "our"), the developer of Serangan Sands in South Lombok, Indonesia. We are responsible for the personal information collected through this website.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiry form: your first and last name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number (optional), what you are interested in, and your message.
  • Meeting bookings: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number (optional), any notes you add, the type of meeting you choose (online or a site tour), the date and time you select, and your time zone.
  • Direct contact: anything you send us by email, WhatsApp or social media.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time of your visit. This is used to keep the site running securely. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we do not build advertising profiles.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you the information you ask for.
  • To send you an automatic email after you contact us, inviting you to book a meeting.
  • To schedule your meeting: creating a calendar event, sending you a calendar invitation and confirmation email and, for online meetings, a Google Meet link.
  • To follow up with you about Serangan Sands and the purchase process, where you have asked us to.
  • To keep records of our dealings with you and meet our legal obligations.
  • To protect the website against spam and misuse.

We will not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We will only send you marketing about future projects if you have agreed to it, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

4. Google services and Google user data

Our booking and enquiry system uses Google services in the following way:

  • Google Calendar: the website checks the free and busy times in the Sol Properties calendar to show available appointment times, and creates an event in that calendar when you book. You are added to the event as a guest, so Google sends you an invitation.
  • Google Meet: online meetings include a Google Meet video link created with the calendar event.
  • Gmail: the website sends enquiry notifications, automatic replies and booking confirmations from the Sol Properties Gmail account.

The website is authorised to access only the Sol Properties Google account. It never asks you to sign in with Google, and it never accesses your own Google account, calendar, contacts or email. Access to the Sol Properties account is limited to: viewing free/busy times, creating calendar events, and sending email. It cannot read the contents of the Sol Properties inbox.

Sol Properties' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the booking and enquiry features described here. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models, and no person reads it except as needed to run the service, for security, or to comply with the law.

When you receive a calendar invitation or join a Google Meet call, Google's own Privacy Policy applies to your use of those Google products.

5. Who we share information with

We share your information only as needed to run this website and deal with your enquiry:

  • Google (Google Calendar, Google Meet and Gmail) to schedule meetings and send emails, as described above.
  • Vercel, our website hosting provider, which processes requests to the website and keeps short-term technical logs.
  • Google Fonts and Google Maps, which load the website's typefaces and the embedded map; your browser connects to Google to load them.
  • Professional advisers such as our lawyers, notaries or accountants, if you proceed with a purchase and it is needed for that transaction.
  • Authorities, where we are required to by law.

These providers may store data outside Indonesia, including in the United States and other countries. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards they provide to protect your information.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking information for as long as we are in contact with you about Serangan Sands, and for up to two years after our last contact, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. If you buy a property, we keep transaction records for as long as the law requires. Hosting logs are kept by our provider for a short period and then deleted automatically.

7. Security

The website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Access to our Google account is protected and limited to the people who need it, and the website's access keys are stored securely with our hosting provider. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022), the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, the Australian Privacy Act 1988 or the GDPR may give you the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • ask us to delete your information or stop using it;
  • withdraw your consent to marketing at any time; and
  • complain to a data protection authority.

To make a request, email us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children

This website is intended for adults considering a property purchase. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
